# Getting a print quote

Two ready-to-send PDFs for print shops, generated by Chitthi itself:

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [`Chitthi-print-specifications.pdf`](Chitthi-print-specifications.pdf) | Every category (postcards, calendars, photo frame prints, fridge magnets, envelopes) with sample designs, the paper and finishing spec, every size with trim, bleed and how many fit per A4 / A3 / 13×19 sheet, and a blank **price per piece** grid for 1, 10, 25, 50, 100, 250 and 500 pieces. Ends with a form for the printer's terms |
| [`Sample-quote-request-postcard.pdf`](Sample-quote-request-postcard.pdf) | What a single design's quote request looks like: previews, the full job spec and its own price grid |

Send the specifications PDF to a few printers; they fill in the grids and return it.

## At export time

Every **print pack** now contains `…-QUOTE-REQUEST.pdf` for that exact design (previews, size, bleed, pages, paper,
weight, finish, colour sides, finishing, envelope and a price grid), and `PRINT-SPEC.txt` lists the same paper and
finishing details. Both documents can also be downloaded on their own from **Print → Get a quote from a print shop**,
the **Specification PDF for printers** button on the sizes guide, or **Find a feature** (Ctrl+K → "quote").

## Paper and finishing per category

| Category | Material | Weight | Finish | Colour | Finishing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Postcards / Instax-style | Art card, matt or uncoated back | 300–350 gsm | Matt or silk, optional front lamination | 4/4 | Trim; 3 mm round corners for Instax sizes |
| Calendars | Art paper, silk or matt; card back page | 170–250 gsm pages, 300 gsm back | Silk or matt | 4/0 | Wire-O top binding with hanger, or desk tent stand |
| Photo frame prints | Photo paper or fine-art matte | 240–300 gsm | Lustre, glossy or matte | 4/0 | Trim exactly to frame size |
| Fridge magnets | Print laminated onto magnetic sheet | 250 gsm + 0.5–0.76 mm magnet | Gloss or matt lamination | 4/0 | Die-cut, 3 mm corners; round sizes as badges |
| Envelopes | Uncoated or kraft | 100–120 gsm (template: 120–160 gsm) | Uncoated | 4/0 | Ready-made envelope, or die-cut and fold the template |

The values live in `src/data/printSpecs.ts`. After changing them, regenerate both PDFs with `npm run dev` running:

```bash
npm run build:quote-docs
```
